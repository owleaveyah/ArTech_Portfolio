THE STORY SO FAR

BRIDGING FINE-ART INTUITION WITH REAL-TIME 3D PIPELINES

3D, Visual Development & Art Direction • Philippines

My work lives at the meeting point of illustration, mood-driven lighting, and real-time 3D worldbuilding.

Starting from 2D illustration and painting in Procreate and Photoshop, I found my voice in how color, silhouette, and surface wear tell a story before a single line of dialogue appears. Moving into 3D allowed me to turn those static mood studies into spaces players can actually walk through—whether a warm cel-shaded Filipino public high school, a cozy hackathon farmstead, or a surreal retro-3D corridor.

Across team sprints and solo experiments, I gravitate toward roles where visual direction and hands-on asset building overlap: shaping the look of modular environments in Blender, painting stylized or PBR materials in Allegorithmic Substance Painter and GIMP, and bringing scenes alive under real-time lighting in Unity and Unreal Engine 5.

CHAPTERS & MILESTONES
01 // VISUAL ROOTS

2D Illustration & Mood

Developing character key art, color keys, and atmospheric studies across Procreate, Adobe Photoshop, and GIMP—anchoring every 3D scene in strong composition and emotional lighting.

02 // TEAM PRODUCTION

Art Direction & Game Jams

Stepping into Art Director, Lead Texture Artist, and Project Hustler roles on collaborative titles like KonTEXTO, Anii! Grow To Know, and See.—uniting modular environments, characters, and playable engine builds.

03 // CURRENT HORIZON

Solo Worldbuilding (IMARA)

Expanding into end-to-end solo vertical slice creation with IMARA in Unreal Engine 5, exploring submerged Philippine archipelago architecture, companion drone concepting, and real-time Lumen lighting.

PRIMARY CRAFT & TOOLS

Blender, Allegorithmic Substance Painter, Procreate, Adobe Photoshop, GIMP — focused on environment art, stylized & PBR texturing, character sheets, and visual development.

FOUNDATIONAL ENGINES & WEB

Unreal Engine 5 (Lumen), Unity Engine, Autodesk Maya, Git & GitHub, Next.js, React Three Fiber / Three.js, Tailwind CSS, HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
